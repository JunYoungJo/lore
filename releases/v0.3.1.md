### Installation

macOS: choose the Apple Silicon (`aarch64`) or Intel (`x64`) DMG and move Lore to Applications. Ad-hoc signing is enabled; Apple Developer ID and notarization are not configured. macOS may still require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to open the app.

Windows: use the `x64-setup.exe` installer. Linux x64: use the AppImage for in-app updates, or install the DEB manually. Existing memory, settings, and update keys are retained.
